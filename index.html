Questions

Before you roll initiative.

Can I play D&D alone with DM Engine?

Yes. DM Engine is an AI Dungeon Master, so you can run a full 5e-style campaign by yourself. It sets each scene, plays the characters you meet and asks for rolls when the story needs them.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. A Guild member can host a game for up to 4 players. Friends join free with a 6-digit code, from a web browser or an iPhone.

Is DM Engine free?

It is free to start. You get 200 Embers when you sign up and free Embers every day you log in. Each DM reply costs 2 Embers. Paid plans start at $7.99 a month.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You can play in your browser at play.dmengineapp.com. There is also an iPhone app on the App Store, and the same account works on both.

Does it follow the 5e rules?

Yes. Ability checks, saving throws, initiative, attack rolls, spell slots, conditions and death saves are all tracked, and you roll a real animated d20.

Is DM Engine an official D&D product?

No. DM Engine is an independent product and is not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. It uses material from the System Reference Document 5.1 under the Creative Commons license.
