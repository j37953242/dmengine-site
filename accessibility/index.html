Accessibility

Built to be played
by everyone.

DM Engine should work for every player, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, larger text or reduced motion. This page says what we have done, what we are still fixing, and how to reach us.

Our goal

The standard we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA on this website and in the game at play.dmengineapp.com, and to follow Apple's accessibility guidance in the iPhone app. We are not fully there yet, and we say below where we fall short.

What we have done

Already in place

Keyboard and structure

Every page can be used with a keyboard. A "Skip to content" link comes first, and pages use landmarks and headings in order.

Readable text

Body text and hints meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Fonts are served from our own site.

Screen readers

Buttons and controls have names. In the game, a new reply from the DM is read aloud on the web, and on iPhone VoiceOver moves to it.

Motion and images

Animations respect your device's reduce-motion setting. Scene pictures are decorative, and the story text describes each scene in full.

Still improving

Known limitations

  • A few warning colors in the game, such as the red Delete buttons, are below the 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
  • On the web, reporting a DM reply needs a long press. Keyboard players can email us instead while we add a keyboard option.
  • Some newer game screens have not yet been fully tested with every screen reader.

Tell us

Found a barrier?

If anything on this site or in the game is hard to use, email support@dmengineapp.com and tell us what happened and what you use (for example, a screen reader or keyboard). We aim to reply within 5 business days, and we can help you with anything in the meantime by email.
