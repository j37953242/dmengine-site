About

Made by one player,
for every table.

DM Engine is an AI Dungeon Master for 5e-style tabletop campaigns. It's made by Jerry, an independent solo developer who loves D&D.

Why it exists

Every story needs a Dungeon Master.

Finding one is the hard part. Schedules clash, groups fall apart, and someone always has to do the prep. DM Engine is built so anyone can sit down and play tonight, alone or with friends, with a DM that knows the rules and remembers the story.

Where you can play

In any modern web browser at play.dmengineapp.com, or in the iPhone app. One account works on both.

What it runs

5e-compatible campaigns with real dice, full combat and four worlds: high fantasy, a hell at war, gothic horror and magic noir.

Languages

English, Spanish and Traditional Chinese.
