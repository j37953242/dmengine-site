FAQ

Before you roll initiative.

Can you play D&D online without a DM?

Yes. DM Engine's AI Dungeon Master runs a 5e-compatible campaign for your whole group online, so nobody has to sit out as the DM.

Do my friends need to pay?

No. Only the host needs a Guild plan. Friends join free with an account and the 6-digit code.

How many players can join?

Up to 4 players in one campaign, including the host.

Can we play on different devices?

Yes. Any mix of computer browsers, phone browsers and the iPhone app can play in the same campaign.

Does it work if nobody knows the rules?

Yes. Everyone just types what their character does. The DM handles checks, initiative and combat, and asks players to roll when it matters.

Does the story carry over between sessions?

Yes. The DM remembers what the party did, who you met and what you broke, and gives a recap when you return.
